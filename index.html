Jack's Fast Break

You picked off a pass and you're a step ahead of everyone. Just you, the goalie, and a defender on your heels.

  • Move with the joystick (or WASD / arrows)
  • Aim the orange target at the goal
  • Hold Shoot to wind up, let go to fire. More power is harder to save but less accurate
  • Aim at the turf for a bounce shot. Goalies hate those
  • Shoot before the defender catches you. 10 breaks a game

Desktop: WASD or arrows to move · mouse to aim · hold click or Space to wind up, release to shoot
